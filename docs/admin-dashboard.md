# Your WordPress admin (Sikshya)

Open **WordPress admin → Sikshya** (your site may show a custom name from branding). You get one full-screen app with a **sidebar**. The labels below match what the plugin ships in code—if you don’t see an item, your **user role** may not have access (students don’t see most of this).

> **Free vs Pro:** Some links show a small **Upgrade** or **Off** badge in the sidebar (same colors as in the product). That means the feature needs **Sikshya Pro**, the right **plan**, and the **Add-on** switched on under **Addons**.

## Sidebar groups (simple map)

Your home inside Sikshya—stats and shortcuts. Start here after [Quick start](/quick-start).
