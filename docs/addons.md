## Which plan unlocks which add-on? {#plan-matrix}

Sikshya Pro ships in three tiers. Each add-on lists its **minimum tier** — anything above unlocks it too.

| Tier | Who it's for | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | Independent creators, single-instructor sites | Reviews & ratings, Instructor dashboard, Content drip, Drip emails, Prerequisites, Calendar, **all 7 premium gateways** |
| **Pro / Growth** | Small academies, paid course shops | Everything in Starter **plus** Bundles, Subscriptions, Multi-instructor, Live classes, SCORM/H5P, Advanced assignments, Question banks, Gradebook, Advanced certificates, Coupons, Dynamic checkout fields, Social login, Analytics exports, Activity log, Branded delivery, Email customisation, CRM automation, Email marketing sync |
| **Scale** | Marketplaces, agencies, enterprise | Everything in Pro / Growth **plus** Webhooks, Zapier, Public API & API keys, Multi-vendor marketplace, White label, Multisite tools, WPML / Weglot multilingual, Enterprise reporting |

> Already on a higher tier? Every add-on a lower tier includes is also yours. Check the per-add-on tier label on each card below.

## How add-ons turn on

<ol class="step-list">
  <li>Install <strong>Sikshya Pro</strong> alongside the free Sikshya plugin.</li>
  <li>Activate your license in <span class="screen-path">Sikshya → License</span>.</li>
  <li>Open <span class="screen-path">Sikshya → Add-ons</span>, search by name or category, click the toggle.</li>
  <li>The relevant admin menus / settings panels appear automatically.</li>
</ol>

> Disabling an add-on hides its UI but **keeps your data** so re-enabling later restores everything.
